HOW TO PLAY

Left stick walks. Push it all the way to run.
Right stick looks around.

HIT swings your weapon. FLASH blasts light and stuns monsters (uses battery). CHALK draws an arrow on the floor so you don't get lost. TAKE appears next to things you can pick up.

Find the EXIT door on every level. The EXIT bars at the top and the beeping get stronger when you are close.

Stay out of the dark and away from monsters, or your SANITY drops. Almond Water fixes everything.

On a keyboard: WASD, arrows, Shift, Space, F, E, C, Esc.
